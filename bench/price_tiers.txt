# bench/apple_svg.py to draw bench/apple-price-tiers.svg.
#
# Prices are approximate US launch prices in dollars and are here for
# orientation only. Two things make them soft:
#
#   - A GPU is not a computer. The NVIDIA rows add a flat 800 USD for a modest
#     host (CPU, board, RAM, PSU, case, storage), so both columns describe a
#     whole machine. Change HOST_USD in apple_svg.py to re-do the arithmetic
#     with a different assumption.
#   - Street prices move, and the RTX 50 series has spent much of its life well
#     above MSRP; the RTX 5090 in particular has traded far above its 1999 USD
#     launch price. Using MSRP therefore flatters the NVIDIA column.
#
# Apple throughputs come from bench/apple.txt, so only the M4 one is measured.
# NVIDIA throughputs come from bench/gpus.txt and are all measured.
#
